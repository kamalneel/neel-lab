An executive’s day runs through a keyboard, a mouse and a phone. HandsFree is software that lets you do it all without your hands.

Where these numbers come from

An illustrative day for a desk-based executive, estimated from these studies. Your day will differ.

  • Email, 2–4 hours a day in most surveys; McKinsey put it at 28% of the work week. McKinsey Global Institute, 2012
  • Meetings, chat and email take 57% of Microsoft 365 time; documents, slides and spreadsheets the other 43%. Microsoft Work Trend Index 2023
  • 117 emails and 153 chat messages a day, and an interruption every 2 minutes. Microsoft, June 2025
  • AI tools: among people who use them, 47% spend 15–59 minutes a day and 32% an hour or more. NBER, 2024
  • ~1,200 app switches a day, about 4 hours a week lost to refocusing. Harvard Business Review, 2022
  • Mouse time runs well above keyboard time in software-logged office studies. Wellnomics
  • Speaking is about 3× faster than typing on a phone. Stanford, 2016

The problem

Too many ways in.
None of them natural.

Type here, click there, tap on the phone, talk to one or two apps. Every system has its own way in.

No uniformity

Every app asks for something different.

Not natural

We bend to the machine, not the other way round.

The natural way

For thousands of years,
work ran on three senses.

See

Read, review, notice.

Speak

Ask, decide, delegate.

Listen

Hear the update, the question, the answer.

  1. Thousands of years See, speak, listen.
  2. The computer age Keyboard and mouse added on top.
  3. Today Everything runs through our hands.

The dependency was never the keyboard.
It was our hands.

HandsFree isn’t against the keyboard. It just doesn’t need your hands.

How it works

One layer, like a keyboard.

A keyboard works with every app because every app expects it. HandsFree is a layer like that, built with AI. You talk to it; it works with everything else.

What you do with it

  • Email
  • Calendar
  • Documents
  • Messages
  • Meetings
  • and the rest of the job

For the modern executive, and anyone whose work happens at a desk.

What it isn’t

  • Not a new laptop. It’s software.
  • Not anti-keyboard. It just frees your hands.
  • Not just dictation. It does the work, not only the typing.
  • Not voice commands, app by app. One layer for all of them.
