The problem

Too many ways in.
None of them natural.

We type with our fingers. We point with a mouse. We tap on glass. A few AI apps let us talk. What comes back is a mix of screens, sounds and short messages. Every system has its own way in.

No uniformity

Keys here, clicks there, a tap on your phone, voice in one or two places. Every app asks for something different.

Not natural

None of it is how people work with each other. We bend to the machine, instead of the other way round.

The natural way

For thousands of years,
work ran on three senses.

See

Read, review, notice.

Speak

Ask, decide, delegate.

Listen

Hear the update, the question, the answer.

  1. Thousands of years See, speak, listen.
  2. The computer age Keyboard and mouse added on top. Eyes, ears and voice still in use.
  3. Today Everything runs through our hands.

The dependency was never the keyboard.
It was our hands.

HandsFree is not against the keyboard. It is a return to the most natural way we know to work: seeing, speaking and listening.

How it works

One layer, like a keyboard.

One keyboard works with every app, because every app is built to expect it. With AI, we can build a new layer like that, one that doesn’t have to be rebuilt for each app. You talk to HandsFree. HandsFree works with everything else.

What you do with it

  • Email
  • Calendar
  • Documents
  • Messages
  • Meetings
  • and the rest of the job

For the modern executive, and anyone whose work happens at a desk.

What it isn’t

  • Not a new laptop. It’s software.
  • Not anti-keyboard. It frees your hands; it doesn’t ban the keys.
  • Not just dictation. It does the work, not only the typing.
  • Not voice commands, app by app. One layer for all of them.
